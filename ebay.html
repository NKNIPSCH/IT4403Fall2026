Where Do Our Cards Come From?

At Ravens Nest Relics, we regularly purchase football card collections from local card shops, individual collectors, private collections, and other suppliers. Every collection is different. Some contain newer releases, others feature older cards, and many include an assortment of products from different manufacturers and years.

By acquiring inventory from multiple sources, we're able to offer something that wouldn't be possible by simply opening a few boxes of one product: a large assortment of football cards representing different eras, brands, players, and styles.

How Do We Build Our Boxes?

Our process is straightforward. We take football cards from different collections, mix them together, and pack them into BCW 500-count storage boxes. We make an effort to include a good variety of players, teams, manufacturers, sets, and years while limiting duplicates whenever practical.

We don't individually handpick cards based on their value when assembling these boxes. Instead, we combine cards from our bulk inventory to create an affordable assortment. Because we're constantly acquiring new collections, our inventory changes regularly, and no two boxes will be exactly alike.

While we try to avoid excessive duplication, duplicates are possible. We also cannot guarantee specific players, teams, sets, or an equal distribution of teams in any particular box.

What Types of Cards Will You Receive?

Our boxes primarily contain standard base cards, but our inventory also includes plenty of rookie cards, star players, veterans, Hall of Famers, inserts, and parallels. The exact mixture depends on the collections used to assemble each box.

We regularly encounter rookie cards and other interesting cards while packing, but we don't track specific percentages or guarantee quantities of any particular card type. Some boxes may contain more rookies or inserts than others. That's simply the nature of combining cards from different collections.

Mostly Modern Football Cards

We make an effort to ensure that the majority of cards in our boxes come from modern football releases. We're not trying to fill storage boxes with nothing but 1990s junk-wax-era cards!

That doesn't mean older cards are excluded. We frequently acquire collections spanning several decades, so you may occasionally find cards from the 1990s, 1980s, or other earlier periods. We believe that a mixture of modern products and occasional older cards makes collecting more interesting.

NFL, College & Unlicensed Cards

The majority of our football card inventory consists of officially licensed NFL products. However, because we purchase collections from different sources, some boxes may also contain college football cards and products without official NFL licensing.

Not every card is guaranteed to feature an NFL uniform or official NFL branding.
