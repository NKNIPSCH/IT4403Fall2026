About Me

My name is Niklas Knipschild, and I am a senior at Kennesaw State University studying Information Technology with a focus on infrastructure. I have been an avid technology geek since I was a little kid. I got my first computer when I was around six or seven years old and built my first computer when I was 11. Since then, I have built every personal computer I have owned, along with several computers for family members and friends over the years.

While in high school, I completed the Cisco CCNA course, which helped build my early interest in networking and IT infrastructure. After high school, I also spent some time selling and building websites. That experience gave me a strong foundation in HTML and CSS, along with some experience using PHP.

Outside of technology, I enjoy collecting sports cards and watching football. I am a huge Baltimore Ravens fan and am still waiting for Lamar Jackson to deliver another Super Bowl to Baltimore. At this point, I would happily settle for a historic playoff run to the Super Bowl, but only time will tell.
